Classifying products

Product line vs. product mix

A product line is a group of closely related products. A product mix is all the lines a company sells. Colgate-Palmolive’s mix has four lines: oral care, personal care, home care and pet nutrition. Oral care alone holds toothpaste, kids’ toothpaste and toothbrushes.

Distribution strategy

Marketing channels make products available when and where customers want to buy them. Middlemen bridge the gap between manufacturer and consumer, creating time, place and ownership utility.

Physical distribution

Logistics moves products to customers. Physical distribution covers transportation (railways, motor vehicles, inland waterways, pipelines, airways), warehousing and materials handling. Transportation creates time and place utility.

Why distribution matters

Distribution decisions are among the least flexible in the mix: they commit resources and set contractual relationships that are slow to change. Online retailing has also raised customer expectations.

Promotion strategy

Promotion works best as integrated marketing communications: coordinating every element of the promotion mix so they act as one unified effort.

Why strategy matters

  • Marketing creates value through the marketing mix.
  • The four elements must be carefully integrated into one effective strategy.
  • Companies with an effective mix gain a competitive advantage, often by excelling at one or more elements.
  • Companies must monitor demand and adapt the mix when necessary.
